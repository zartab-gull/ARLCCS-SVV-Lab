| Constraint ID | Constraint in Simple English | Why the Constraint is Necessary |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | The barrier must not open while a train is present at the crossing. | Prevents road traffic from entering the crossing while a train is passing. |
| C2 | The barrier must close when an approaching train is detected. | Prevents vehicles from entering the crossing before the train arrives. |
| C3 | Warning signals must be activated when an approaching train is detected. | Warns road users that a train is approaching. |
| C4 | The barrier must remain closed while the train is passing through the crossing. | Prevents road traffic from entering the crossing during train movement. |
| C5 | The barrier must open only after the system confirms that the train has completely cleared the crossing. | Prevents premature opening of the crossing. |
| C6 | The system must not allow normal road traffic when a train is present at the crossing. | Prevents collision between road traffic and the train. |
| C7 | The system must not open the barrier when train-detection sensor readings are incorrect or unreliable. | Prevents unsafe barrier opening based on incorrect information. |
| C8 | The system must respond safely when a barrier failure is detected. | Prevents unsafe operation when the physical barrier cannot operate correctly. |
| C9 | The system must maintain a safe crossing condition when communication with the control center is lost. | Prevents communication failure from causing an unsafe crossing state. |
| C10 | Emergency conditions must cause the system to enter a safe operating condition. | Ensures safety during abnormal or emergency situations. |
